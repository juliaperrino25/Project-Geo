# World Map Snapshots

Project Geo ships two historical snapshots of the world map: **1 January 1914**, the last New Year before the First World War, and **1 January 1940**, the last New Year before Germany's invasions of Denmark, Norway, the Low Countries, and France. Each snapshot has its own set of map entities, historical notes, and quiz defaults. The sections below cover the two snapshots in turn, followed by limitations that apply to both.

## 1 January 1914

### Why This Snapshot?

The date **1 January 1914** captures the last New Year of the long nineteenth century, seven months before the outbreak of the First World War:

- **Europe's four land empires are still intact**: the German, Austro-Hungarian, Russian, and Ottoman Empires all still rule their prewar extents; none will survive the coming war in its 1914 form
- **The Balkan Wars have just concluded** (1912–13): the Ottoman Empire has lost nearly all its European territory; Serbia and Greece have roughly doubled in size; Bulgaria has gained Western Thrace but lost Southern Dobruja to Romania; Albania is independent for the first time, since 1913
- **Austria-Hungary annexed Bosnia-Herzegovina outright in 1908**, a move that embittered Serbia and set the stage for the crisis that follows the assassination at Sarajevo later this year
- **The German colonial empire is at its full territorial extent**: German East Africa, German South-West Africa, Kamerun, Togoland, German New Guinea, and German Samoa are all still German; all will be lost by 1919
- **Ireland is still fully part of the United Kingdom**, with the Home Rule crisis at its height
- **Morocco has been a French protectorate only since 1912**, and Italy has held Libya and the Dodecanese Islands from the Ottomans since the same year
- **The Qing dynasty has just fallen** (1911): China is a fragile young republic, and Tibet and Mongolia are both effectively independent since 1912
- **Korea has been annexed by Japan** (1910), and Norway has been independent of Sweden since 1905
- **The Panama Canal is nearly complete**, due to open in August 1914

This snapshot is a frozen moment before the July Crisis and the guns of August reshape the map entirely.

### Simplifications on This Map

To keep the quiz playable while respecting 1914 borders, the map makes these intentional simplifications:

1. **Austria-Hungary** is drawn as a single territory, not as its Austrian and Hungarian halves or its constituent crown lands

2. **The Ottoman Empire** is drawn as one territory including its Arabian provinces; the Nejd emirate in the interior is shown separately

3. **Kamerun** is drawn at its pre-1911 extent, so the "Neukamerun" strips ceded by France in 1911 are not shown

4. **Togoland and the Gold Coast** use the modern Togo/Ghana border, so the part of Togoland that later went to Ghana is not shown separately

5. **Tuva** is shown inside Mongolia; it became a Russian protectorate in April 1914, a few months after this snapshot

6. **The border between British Papua and German New Guinea** is an approximation drawn at map scale, not the surveyed line

7. **The Panama Canal Zone, the Kiautschou leased territory in China, and other small leased or internationalized areas** are not shown separately

8. **Egypt** is shown as a British-occupied entity though it remained nominally Ottoman until December 1914

9. **Cyprus** is shown as British though it too remained nominally Ottoman until 1914

## 1 January 1940

### Why This Snapshot?

The date **1 January 1940** captures a pivotal moment in world history:

- **Poland has already fallen** (partitioned between Nazi Germany and the Soviet Union in September 1939)
- **Winter War underway** (Finland defending against Soviet invasion)
- **Czechoslovakia dismembered** (March 1939); Bohemia-Moravia under German control, Slovakia a German puppet
- **Axis taking shape**: Italy is bound to Germany by the Pact of Steel (May 1939) but still non-belligerent; Japan controls Manchuria (as Manchukuo) and much of coastal China
- **Colonial empires still intact**: British, French, Dutch, Portuguese, Spanish, and Belgian empires at their territorial peak
- **Western neutrals still intact**: Denmark, Norway, the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg are independent and neutral; all will be invaded by Germany in April–May 1940
- **France still unoccupied**: The collapse of France and Vichy division are 6 months away
- **United States neutral**: Will not enter the war until December 1941
- **Soviet Union and Germany at uneasy peace**: After the Nazi-Soviet Pact (August 1939)

This snapshot is a frozen moment before the Blitzkrieg reshapes Europe and the Pacific Theater explodes.

### Simplifications on This Map

To keep the quiz playable while respecting 1940 borders, the map makes these intentional simplifications:

1. **Bohemia-Moravia and Sudetenland** are shown as integral parts of Germany (absorbed March 1939)

2. **Polish territories** annexed by Germany and the USSR are shown as one **General Government** entity, even though they had different administrative status

3. **Japanese-occupied China** (Manchukuo, Kwantung Leased Territory, and zones of occupation) is shown simply as **China**, with Manchukuo as a separate puppet state

4. **Italian territories** unified in **Italian East Africa** (Ethiopia, Eritrea, Somalia) are shown as one entity, conquered and unified in 1936

5. **British Cameroons** are shown administratively with **Nigeria** rather than as a separate Mandate territory, reflecting the practical administration at the time

6. **Small islands and microstates** (Monaco, Liechtenstein, San Marino, Bahrain, Maldives, Mauritius, etc.) are included on the map but excluded from the quiz (`quiz: false`) because they are too small to click reliably at world-map scale

7. **First Vienna Award** territories (southern Slovakia and Carpathian Ruthenia, awarded to Hungary in November 1938) are shown as part of Hungary

8. **Memel** (transferred from Lithuania to Germany in March 1939) is shown as part of Germany

9. **Greater Romania** is shown with Bessarabia, Bukovina, and Southern Dobruja—territories Romania will be forced to cede to the Soviet Union and Bulgaria in June 1940

10. **Vilnius** is shown as part of Lithuania (transferred October 1939, shortly before this snapshot)

## Known Limitations

These apply to both snapshots:

- **Borders are approximate** and rendered at world-map scale; small territories and border regions may be simplified or exaggerated for visibility
- **Colonial possessions** are shown with their political sovereign (e.g., Greenland as "Greenland (Danish colony)" under Denmark), reflecting the legal sovereignty of the time
- **Puppet states and occupied territories** are labeled as such where applicable, but should not be confused with sovereign independence
- **Disputed territories** (like parts of Siberia, Inner Mongolia, or border regions) follow the de facto control as of each snapshot's date (1 January 1914 or 1 January 1940)
- **Maritime borders** are not shown; only land boundaries are depicted
