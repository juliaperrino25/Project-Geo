# World Map as of 1 January 1940

## Why This Snapshot?

The date **1 January 1940** captures a pivotal moment in world history:

- **Poland has already fallen** (partitioned between Nazi Germany and the Soviet Union in September 1939)
- **Winter War underway** (Finland defending against Soviet invasion)
- **Czechoslovakia dismembered** (March 1939); Bohemia-Moravia under German control, Slovakia a German puppet
- **Axis taking shape**: Italy is bound to Germany by the Pact of Steel (May 1939) but still non-belligerent; Japan controls Manchuria (as Manchukuo) and much of coastal China
- **Colonial empires still intact**: British, French, Dutch, Portuguese, Spanish, and Belgian empires at their territorial peak
- **Western neutrals still intact**: Denmark, Norway, the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg are independent and neutral; all will be invaded by Germany in April–May 1940
- **France still unoccupied**: The collapse of France and Vichy division are 6 months away
- **United States neutral**: Will not enter the war until December 1941
- **Soviet Union and Germany at uneasy peace**: After the Nazi-Soviet Pact (August 1939)

This snapshot is a frozen moment before the Blitzkrieg reshapes Europe and the Pacific Theater explodes.

## Simplifications on This Map

To keep the quiz playable while respecting 1940 borders, the map makes these intentional simplifications:

1. **Bohemia-Moravia and Sudetenland** are shown as integral parts of Germany (absorbed March 1939)

2. **Polish territories** annexed by Germany and the USSR are shown as one **General Government** entity, even though they had different administrative status

3. **Japanese-occupied China** (Manchukuo, Kwantung Leased Territory, and zones of occupation) is shown simply as **China**, with Manchukuo as a separate puppet state

4. **Italian territories** unified in **Italian East Africa** (Ethiopia, Eritrea, Somalia) are shown as one entity, conquered and unified in 1936

5. **British Cameroons** are shown administratively with **Nigeria** rather than as a separate Mandate territory, reflecting the practical administration at the time

6. **Small islands and microstates** (Monaco, Liechtenstein, San Marino, Bahrain, Maldives, Mauritius, etc.) are included on the map but excluded from the quiz (`quiz: false`) because they are too small to click reliably at world-map scale

7. **First Vienna Award** territories (southern Slovakia and Carpathian Ruthenia, awarded to Hungary in November 1938) are shown as part of Hungary

8. **Memel** (transferred from Lithuania to Germany in March 1939) is shown as part of Germany

9. **Greater Romania** is shown with Bessarabia, Bukovina, and Southern Dobruja—territories Romania will be forced to cede to the Soviet Union and Bulgaria in June 1940

10. **Vilnius** is shown as part of Lithuania (transferred October 1939, shortly before this snapshot)

## Known Limitations

- **Borders are approximate** and rendered at world-map scale; small territories and border regions may be simplified or exaggerated for visibility
- **Colonial possessions** are shown with their political sovereign (e.g., Greenland as "Greenland (Danish colony)" under Denmark), reflecting the legal sovereignty of the time
- **Puppet states and occupied territories** are labeled as such where applicable, but should not be confused with sovereign independence
- **Disputed territories** (like parts of Siberia, Inner Mongolia, or border regions) follow the de facto control as of January 1940
- **Maritime borders** are not shown; only land boundaries are depicted
